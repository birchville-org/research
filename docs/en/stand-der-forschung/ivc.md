# State of Research: Indus Valley Culture (IVC)

::: note-box [Living Synthesis Dashboard]
This page documents the **cumulative state of scientific research** on the Bronze Age Indus Civilization (*Indus Valley Culture / Harappan Civilization*, c. 3300–1300 BCE). Updated following each monthly report, it synthesizes empirical findings across archaeology, archaeogenetics, epigraphy, and paleoclimatology.
:::

## 1. Disciplinary Evidence Matrix

| Research Domain | Scholarly Consensus / Prevailing Trend | Research Status | Confidence Level |
|:----------------|:---------------------------------------|:----------------|:-----------------|
| **Archaeology & Urbanism** | Monumental urban fortifications (e.g. 7-meter mudbrick perimeter wall at Mohenjo-Daro) date back to the pre-classical Kot Diji phase (c. 3300–2600 BCE). Continuity of regional craft and maritime infrastructure (Lothal, Lakhapar) into the post-urban period (after 1900 BCE). | **Established / Revision of Wheeler's flood dike hypothesis** | High (AMS-14C series & stratigraphy) |
| **Archaeogenetics & Palaeoanthropology** | Genetic core profile of the IVC population is modeled on a two-stream cline: autochthonous South Asian hunter-gatherers (AASI) plus a basal Zagros/Iran-Neolithic-related lineage. Complete absence of Steppe ancestry (*Steppe_MLBA*) and Anatolian farmer gene flow in mature urban samples (I6113, Indus Periphery). | **Consensus model (Reich/Shinde et al.)** | High (Multi-genome sequencing datasets) |
| **Epigraphy & Script Research** | Statistical NLP and conditional entropy models refute the non-linguistic symbol thesis (Farmer/Witzel), demonstrating rigid syntactic positional constraints. The Rebus principle based on Proto-Dravidian remains the leading working hypothesis, yet requires bilingual verification. | **Controversial / Methodological refinement** | Moderate (Lack of a Rosetta Stone) |
| **Paleoclimatology & Environmental History** | Speleothems (Dharamjali Cave) and lake sediment cores (BSIP) demonstrate multidecadal drought cycles (4.2k event) and an uninterrupted 113-year megadrought (c. 1580–1470 BCE). Instead of sudden collapse, an organized deurbanization occurred: crop switching to summer millets and eastward relocation into the Ganga-Yamuna Doab. | **Strongly supported** | Very High (Quantitative climate proxies) |

## 2. Ongoing Research Projects

Key ongoing field excavations and laboratory pipelines with publications expected in upcoming cycles:

::: grammar-box
1. **Rakhigarhi Mound 7 Multi-Isotope & aDNA Pipeline:**
   - **Institutions:** *Anthropological Survey of India (AnSI, Kolkata)* & *Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences (BSIP, Lucknow)* in cooperation with ASI.
   - **Scope:** Analytical processing of three complete skeletons recovered in 2025/26. Strontium (87Sr/86Sr) and oxygen stable isotope assays to reconstruct individual geographic origins and mobility.
   - **Relevance:** Statistically expands the genomic and paleodemographic baseline beyond earlier single-sample constraints.

2. **Mohenjo-Daro Chronology & Pre-Classical Stratigraphy:**
   - **Institutions:** *Directorate General of Antiquities Sindh* & *SEAS Pakistan* (J. M. Kenoyer).
   - **Scope:** Expanded AMS-14C dating series along the western Stupa mound edge to delineate the Kot Diji to Mature Harappan transition.

3. **National Maritime Heritage Complex (NMHC) & Lothal Investigations:**
   - **Institution:** *Archaeological Survey of India (ASI)*.
   - **Scope:** Core drillings and restudy of the tidal basin, drainage conduits, and semi-precious bead manufacturing sectors.

4. **Iravatham Mahadevan Computational Epigraphy Initiative:**
   - **Sponsor:** *Department of Archaeology, Government of Tamil Nadu*.
   - **Scope:** Complete digital corpus ingestion and standardized sign datasets for multimodal AI and NLP decipherment algorithms.
:::

## 3. Open Scientific Controversies & Desiderata

Crucial questions remaining unresolved in international research:

::: note-box [Open Research Questions]
- **Decipherment of the Indus Script:**
  Without the discovery of a bilingual text (Egyptian/Mesopotamian style), linguistic assignment (Proto-Dravidian vs. Munda vs. an extinct isolate) remains provisional until external proper names can be verified in Western Asiatic records.
- **Paleohydrology of the Ghaggar-Hakra River System:**
  The precise chronology of the transition from a perennial glacial-fed river system to a monsoon-fed ephemeral drainage network (and its causative role in deurbanization) continues to be debated among geoscientists and archaeologists.
- **Absence of Royal Necropolises and Palatial Architecture:**
  Unlike Mesopotamia and Egypt, the IVC exhibits no monumental royal tombs or hereditary palaces. The underlying sociopolitical architecture (heterarchy, corporate councils, or civic-religious guilds) remains an open debate.
:::
