# State of Research: OIT vs. AMT

::: note-box [Living Synthesis Dashboard]
This page tracks the **cumulative scientific consensus and ongoing debates** between the *Out-of-India Theory (OIT)* and the *Aryan Migration Theory (AMT / Steppe model)*. It is synthesized continuously after each monthly research digest.
:::

## 1. Disciplinary Evidence Matrix

| Discipline | Scientific Consensus & Trends | AMT Status | OIT Status | Confidence |
|:-----------|:------------------------------|:-----------|:-----------|:-----------|
| **Archaeogenetics (aDNA)** | Steppe ancestry (*Steppe_MLBA*, Sintashta-associated) arrives in NW South Asia between 1900–1500 BCE, strictly correlating with Y-haplogroup R1a-Z93/L657. Completely absent in mature IVC individuals. No reverse Bronze Age gene flow from India into the Pontic steppe. | **Strongly Supported** | **Refuted** (strong Out-of-India expansion) | High (Multi-genome coverage) |
| **Archaeozoology** | Domesticated horses (*Equus caballus*) and spoked-wheel chariots appear post-1900 BCE; early claims (Surkotada) remain unconfirmed by osteometry. Earliest spoked chariots emerge in Sintashta (ca. 2000 BCE). | **Supported** | **Uncorroborated** | Medium-High |
| **Historical Linguistics** | Rigvedic Sanskrit displays non-IE substrates (BMAC, Dravidian, Austroasiatic/Munda). The 4-way stop series is an areal innovation. Separation of Indo-Aryan and Iranian dated to 2000–1800 BCE. | **Consensus Paradigm** | **Incompatible** with deep chronology (>4000 BCE) | High (Phylolinguistics & dialectology) |
| **Indology & Philology** | Historical-critical stratification of the Rigveda (early family books II–VII vs late books I & X) reflects eastward movement from Swat/Punjab to the Ganga-Yamuna Doab. Sarasvati identified with the seasonal Ghaggar-Hakra system. | **Compatible** | **Inconsistent** with astronomical hyper-dating | Medium-High |

## 2. Ongoing Research Projects

Active laboratory and field initiatives expected to release critical data in upcoming cycles:

::: grammar-box
1. **Rakhigarhi Mound 7 aDNA & Multi-Isotope Suite:**
   - **Institutions:** *Anthropological Survey of India (AnSI)* & *Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences (BSIP)* with ASI.
   - **Scope:** Complete aDNA attempts plus Strontium (87Sr/86Sr) and Oxygen enamel ratios on newly excavated burials to assess geographic mobility.
   - **Significance:** Expands the extremely small IVC genomic reference baseline (previously just I6113).

2. **ASI Publication Mandate for Key Sites:**
   - **Institution:** *Archaeological Survey of India (ASI)*.
   - **Scope:** Enforcing strict deadlines for delayed comprehensive excavation reports on **Rakhigarhi** and **Sinauli** (OCP-period solid-wheel carts/chariots).

3. **Syrian Euphrates Contact Horizon:**
   - **Institutions:** BHU (Gyaneshwer Chaubey) with Polish research partners.
   - **Scope:** Formal peer-reviewed publication of mitochondrial haplogroups (M4b1, M49, M61) from Tell Ashara / Tell Masaikh teeth.

4. **Ghaggar-Hakra Paleoclimate Core Profiles:**
   - **Institution:** BSIP Lucknow.
   - **Scope:** High-resolution monsoon and alluvial sedimentation reconstruction tracking the desiccation timeline (4.2k event).
:::

## 3. Open Issues & Scholarly Desiderata

Methodological bottlenecks and contested domains currently debated in the literature:

::: note-box [Open Questions]
- **Equid Osteometric Discrimination:**
  Unambiguous morphological and genomic separation between *Equus hemionus* (onager/khur), *Equus asinus*, and true domestic *Equus caballus* in pre-1800 BCE NW South Asia.
- **Missing aDNA from the Ganga-Yamuna Doab (OCP & PGW):**
  Lack of high-coverage ancient DNA from Painted Grey Ware (PGW) and Ochre Coloured Pottery (OCP) horizons that represent the late Bronze / early Iron Age transition.
- **BMAC Substrate Linguistic Identification:**
  Accurate phonetic and morphological reconstruction of the non-Indo-European contact language underlying early Indo-Iranian loans.
- **Rigvedic Astronomical vs. Textual Chronology:**
  Resolving tensions between astronomical dating models and historical-critical internal stratification.
:::
