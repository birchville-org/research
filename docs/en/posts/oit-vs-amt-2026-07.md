# OIT vs. AMT: Monthly Report July 2026

::: note-box [Executive Summary]
July 2026 saw key insights from a peer-reviewed population genetic study on the Sindhi diaspora alongside major institutional breakthroughs in skeletal sample processing from the mature Harappan necropolis of Rakhigarhi.
:::

## 1. Disciplinary Evidence Matrix

| Research Domain | Observed Dataset | Implication for AMT | Implication for OIT | Confidence |
|:----------------|:-----------------|:--------------------|:--------------------|:-----------|
| **Population Genetics (Sindhis)** | Devnani et al. (*Human Genetics*, 2026) | Strongly consistent with Steppe-pulse models across NW South Asia. | Contradicts pan-Indian autochthonous homogeneity. | High (Peer-Reviewed) |
| **Archaeology & aDNA (Rakhigarhi)** | Burials from Mound 7 handed over to AnSI | Reduces reliance on single-individual proxies once sequenced. | Ongoing project; results pending. | Ongoing Project |

## 2. Key Synthesis

The Sindhi genomic findings confirm that northwestern South Asian populations reflect substantial Bronze Age gene flow from the steppe, overlaid onto an indigenous Indus-related agricultural substrate.
