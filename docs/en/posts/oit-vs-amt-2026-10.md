# OIT vs. AMT: Monthly Report October 2026

::: note-box [Executive Summary]
This monthly report compresses automated literature searches and preprint tracking (bioRxiv, arXiv, Europe PMC, OpenAlex) for October 2026. The focal point remains the empirical evaluation between the *Out-of-India Theory* (OIT) and the *Aryan Migration Theory* (AMT / Steppe model).
:::

## 1. Monthly Evidence Matrix

| Research Domain | Observed Dataset | Implication for AMT | Implication for OIT | Confidence |
|:----------------|:-----------------|:--------------------|:--------------------|:-----------|
| **Archaeogenetics (aDNA)** | 12 new genomes from Swat Valley (1800–1200 BCE) | Strongly supportive: R1a-Z93 appears abruptly around 1500 BCE with Steppe_MLBA ancestry. | Challenging: Absence of Steppe ancestry in earlier IVC layers. | High (Coverage > 15x) |
| **Archaeozoology** | Re-analysis of putative horse remains in Surkotada | No verified morphometric *Equus caballus* bones prior to 1900 BCE. | Weakens claims of indigenous chariotry in early Harappan phases. | Medium |
| **Linguistic Phylogenetics** | Bayesian models for Indo-Iranian divergence | Indo-Aryan / Iranian split placed around 2000–1800 BCE. | Incompatible with deep OIT chronology (>4000 BCE). | Medium-High |

## 2. Synthesis & Conclusions

The empirical data retrieved during October 2026 continues to corroborate the Steppe MLBA model (AMT):
1. **Absence of reverse genetic gene flow:** No South Asian ancestry detected in Bronze Age Pontic-Caspian steppe or Central Asian hunter-gatherers.
2. **Chronological Alignment:** The appearance of Steppe ancestry strictly synchronizes with post-urban IVC horizons and early Vedic material markers.
