# OIT vs. AMT: Monthly Report June 2026

::: note-box [Executive Summary]
June 2026 was marked by announcements of new skeletal excavations at mature Harappan Rakhigarhi rather than newly published ancient DNA datasets. The Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) confirmed the recovery of eight skeletons, which have entered analytical pipelines at BSIP Lucknow.
:::
