## Project Affiliation & Purpose

The **Birchville Blog** (`blog.birchville.org`) is part of the private project **[birchville.org](https://birchville.org)**. All published contents serve exclusively private and scholarly documentation purposes.

The research reports and digests published on this platform are based on systematic evaluation, synthesis, and compression of automated literature and data surveys. "Automated" signifies that sources are selected according to predefined quality criteria, but have not undergone further manual review or verification by qualified experts.
