::: details What does "OIT vs. AMT" mean? (Out of India vs. Aryan Migration Theory)
**OIT vs. AMT** compares the empirical models of the **Out of India Theory (OIT)** and the **Aryan Migration Theory (AMT / Steppe Hypothesis)**:

- **OIT (Out of India Theory):** Proposes that the Indo-European language family originated within the Indian subcontinent and subsequently expanded westward across Eurasia.
- **AMT (Aryan Migration Theory):** Represents the linguistic and archaeogenetic consensus model, positing that Indo-Iranian speakers migrated into northwestern South Asia from the Eurasian steppe via Central Asia during the 2nd millennium BCE.
:::
